This weekend I didn't expect to get one of my goals in L4D2 to be achieved because I expect it will take month or two to make it happen mainly either my teammates is inexperienced or I can't keep up with the map. Lets start with the former.

# The Struggle to achieve 100 with Local Teams.
In my journey during survival I always play with Asia or Aussie player (APAC) and rarely play with my own countrymen because in 2022-2023 is rare someone want to play survival, it because most of them only play Campaign or Versus and Survival is another category for them.

Back then Me and my playmate finally assemble first teams with all local players and try to play Sugarmill, we play decently back then and managed to get 50 minutes from there the spark to get 100 minutes is started. But it wasn't smooth journey since most of us have own thing in real life we rarely play as teams in daily basis and most of our attempt is basically one hit wonder.

Some map we play is Sugarmill, Float, and Street. Sugarmill is depedent on aim and it need strong player at front and the back, in one of our attempt the back player have issue with his FPS and it cap at 60 when usually is 120 and it make his performance worse than usual. We ended earlier because he can't keep up with constant CI spawn in there. 

We switch map with Street because the strategy is the same and hopefully he can keep up even with his issue, we managed to get 97 minutes in Street and is impresive of him to able keep up with brand new map we rarely attempt as teams.

Finally is Float, Float has been special for me because I remember carry newbie in this map for 60 minutes when the original attempt was to get 30 minutes but sadly he didn't get the record because disconnected in the game. And this map I really train how to bait Tank and to make sure not get hit while gunning it down.

I recall about 3 or 4 attempt we did in this map as Full ID teams, our first attempt was ambitious where one of our teammate goes to internet cafe and play there not only for L4D2 but also Valorant. It was scorching hot because we play around 13:00 to 15:00 and we have two attempt in that map, we only managed [44 minutes](http://survival.site.nfoservers.com/DemoArchive/index.php?id=31046) and [77 Minutes](http://survival.site.nfoservers.com/DemoArchive/index.php?id=31043). It so dissapointing to go this far but still failed to get 100 minutes, this games can be cruel sometimes.

Our third attempt with another teammate and he play as bait, back then my laptop monitor is broken and I can't barely see anything in front but I still try to play. We only managed [94](http://survival.site.nfoservers.com/DemoArchive/index.php?id=151075) minutes because we choke and the bait player seems doesn't have much experience in the Float as bait.

I have mention in [May]({% post_url 2026-05-17-may-pb-l4d2 %}) that I am hiatus from L4D2 about two years and last time I have serious attempt was in Float with broken monitor, after coming back from internship and training rigorously in GYM while bulking I really went off with my endurance and get [5 hours in Bus Depot](https://youtu.be/azaWd-JwCjk) as result and this trend really help me to improve for this weeks Survival sessions.

We have some new face joining the Survival Fry and one of them got potential to be strong player in future, since they are inexperienced I expect it will take some time for them get certain level to able to comfortably play survival.

# 100 Minutes in Terminal
Oh boy Terminal or Dead Air Chapter 3 in Campaign, this map have special memory for me. In the past I have attempted this map several time and my recent highest was 96 minutes but that record was flawed because my teams did pause the game twice with admin command and I also vote to invoke the pause for pee break also during the run we ran out of the kits. We did Kit Run attempt and I was initiating it first but make mistake with the break timing and screw up in the process so that's why I make stand to beat Terminal.

This weeks I joining with the newbies in Survival and while waiting for more playres to join I did 65 minutes lap in Cemetery duo, and it was really boring cause we rarely shoot the SI so the random element is tone down. 

In the process I use auto shotgun and try to practice lining up my shoot with CI to maximize the DPS and reduce the need for reload somehow that technique cames handy in the Terminal round, after that we did full teams in Cemetery and finally switching to Terminal.

Playing Terminal with newbies is expected for the result is ass, they barely have practice in each position so we switch a lot and then they struggle defend themselves againts common infected let alone Tank or SI, I did support several times to help baiter but they get hit alot by Tank. But at one point they seems to settle can hold out for about 20 minutes but still frustated because the strategy doesn't work. Me and My friend who spectating said that is normal and doesn't matter how you tweak strategy if you play bad then the result will bad. 

Then he joined to substitute another player and this where got interesting, this time I bait and I know for sure they can't play bait for shit and still insisting they will bait but **NO**. I sure if they bait we won't get 100 and only manage 40 or 50.

The round goes smoothly and I even try to improvise how I play, in the past I will take another shotgun to me and only baiting with ammo I have and try to make room for the support bait. This time I try to be more aggressive toward the front and capitalize the shotgun spawn for more DPS with Tank. The result was I got friendly fire lot in the first 30 minutes but it dies down in 30+ minutes and only occasionally.

Also this time the Kit Run goes much better with better timing in the Tank and manage to get 3 kits, even though I have to get charged for 2 times in the row but the round was success with overall performance on point in each post.

With Terminal 100 minutes in marks as the **First Full Local Teams** in Indonesia ever recorded and it was my dreams to be able compete with full local players for 100 minutes in Survival, and this also special cause the 2 player still relatively new in Survival and the map also is intense than any other maps to try for 100 minutes.

# Beating the hardest map in the game
Day after that I though about to take break from the game but my playmate inviting me to play and to my suprise it was stacked with good players and indeed he want another shoot in the hardest map in the game which is Rooftop.

In my Experience what make Rooftop is hard are there is order in that map, only chaos minutes after minutes. What I mean is in every map there is tempo or rhythm that make each wave is can be deal with the players like Terminal for example if the tank comes and got killed for 15-20 second then the team came recover and anticipating another tank, in the even of two tank the baiter can try to lure one while the other tank can throw rock or follow the player even if the tank still High HP it can be killed switfly without taking any damage.

In the Rooftop Tank can Spawn in place far like in helipad or near the teams like behind or unexpected place combine that with relentless Common and Special Infected sorrouding the teams it really test the mental and skills for each player. That's why is so chaos in Rooftop with so much thing can go wrong and the punishment are also severe if play badly.

The teams I played are the strongest in Asia that has been grinding the hardest map in the past like Plantation and Port Sacrifice and the only map left is Rooftop, I didn't participate in their grind because I didn't played the games during the period but also the fact that I am still using potato laptop while the teams using good gears make me insecure and thinking "can I keep up with them?" in the even I got invited.
