This weekend I didn't expect to get one of my goals in L4D2 to be achieved because I expect it will take month or two to make it happen mainly either my teammates is inexperienced or I can't keep up with the map. Lets start with the former.

# The Struggle to achieve 100 with Local Teams.
In my journey during survival I always play with Asia or Aussie player (APAC) and rarely play with my own countrymen because in 2022-2023 is rare someone want to play survival, it because most of them only play Campaign or Versus and Survival is another category for them.

Back then Me and my playmate finally assemble first teams with all local players and try to play Sugarmill, we play decently back then and managed to get 50 minutes from there the spark to get 100 minutes is started. But it wasn't smooth journey since most of us have own thing in real life we rarely play as teams in daily basis and most of our attempt is basically one hit wonder.

Some map we play is Sugarmill, Float, and Street. Sugarmill is depedent on aim and it need strong player at front and the back, in one of our attempt the back player have issue with his FPS and it cap at 60 when usually is 120 and it make his performance worse than usual. We ended earlier because he can't keep up with constant CI spawn in there. 

We switch map with Street because the strategy is the same and hopefully he can keep up even with his issue, we managed to get 97 minutes in Street and is impresive of him to able keep up with brand new map we rarely attempt as teams.

Finally is Float, Float has been special for me because I remember carry newbie in this map for 60 minutes when the original attempt was to get 30 minutes but sadly he didn't get the record because disconnected in the game. And this map I really train how to bait Tank and to make sure not get hit while gunning it down.

I recall about 3 or 4 attempt we did in this map as Full ID teams, our first attempt was ambitious where one of our teammate goes to internet cafe and play there not only for L4D2 but also Valorant. It was scorching hot because we play around 13:00 to 15:00 and we have two attempt in that map, we only managed [44 minutes](http://survival.site.nfoservers.com/DemoArchive/index.php?id=31046) and [77 Minutes](http://survival.site.nfoservers.com/DemoArchive/index.php?id=31043). It so dissapointing to go this far but still failed to get 100 minutes, this games can be cruel sometimes.

Our third attempt with another teammate and he play as bait, back then my laptop monitor is broken and I can't barely see anything in front but I still try to play. We only managed [94](http://survival.site.nfoservers.com/DemoArchive/index.php?id=151075) minutes because we choke and the bait player seems doesn't have much experience in the Float as bait.

I have mention in [May]({% post_url 2026-05-17-may-pb-l4d2 %}) that I am hiatus from L4D2 about two years and last time I have serious attempt was in Float with broken monitor, after coming back from internship and training rigorously in GYM while bulking I really went off with my endurance and get [5 hours in Bus Depot](https://youtu.be/azaWd-JwCjk) as result and this trend really help me to improve for this weeks Survival sessions.
